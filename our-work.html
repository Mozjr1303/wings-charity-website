WHAT WE DO & HOW WE WORK

Giving Vulnerable Newborns a Chance at Life

We don’t support all babies. Wings is an international UK-based charity led by women passionate about supporting fellow mothers in need. We step in specifically where a mother cannot care for her newborn due to medical complications, coma, severe illness, or lack of mental capacity.

Our Core Operating Criteria

Our support is focused exclusively on newborns whose mothers are facing life-threatening medical conditions—including coma, severe postpartum illness, postpartum haemorrhage (PPH), eclampsia, lack of mental capacity, or inability to produce milk in extreme poverty. We work directly with government hospital staff in Malawi to vet and verify every single case.

OPERATIONAL PILLAR 01

Government Hospital Identification & Clinical Vetting

To ensure aid reaches those in the most desperate clinical and financial circumstances, Wings partners directly with government maternity hospitals and referral centres in Malawi.

Our team works hand-in-hand with obstetric doctors, midwives, and ward nurses to identify eligible newborns immediately upon delivery. Hospital staff refer cases that meet our strict eligibility criteria, ensuring our interventions are targeted with clinical rigor.

  • Maternal Coma & Critical ICU Care: Newborns whose mothers remain unconscious or fighting for life following traumatic delivery complications.
  • PPH & Eclampsia Complications: Severe postpartum haemorrhage, organ failure, or postpartum seizures leaving the mother physically unable to breastfeed or nurse.
  • Maternal Mental Incapacity: Mothers experiencing acute postpartum psychiatric conditions or lacking cognitive capacity to safely nourish and safeguard their child.
  • Lactation Failure in Extreme Poverty: Verified cases where the mother cannot produce milk and the family cannot afford infant formula, risking neonatal starvation.
OPERATIONAL PILLAR 02

Dedicated Private Nannies in the Home

When a new mother is comatose, hospitalized, or physically incapacitated, the burden of newborn care abruptly falls upon an elderly grandmother, an aunt, or an overwhelmed father who must also earn daily subsistence.

Wings recruits, vets, and pays for dedicated private nannies to work directly in the home. Our nannies provide continuous, skilled infant care—feeding, bathing, comforting, and safeguarding the newborn—so the mother can focus entirely on medical recovery.

  • Round-the-Clock Infant Attention: Ensuring feedings occur every 2 to 3 hours with rigorous hygiene and sterilisation practices.
  • Relief for Extended Kinship Caregivers: Relieving vulnerable grandmothers of physical exhaustion while keeping the newborn within family care.
  • Clinical Danger-Sign Vigilance: Trained to recognize neonatal jaundice, respiratory distress, and fever for immediate clinical intervention.
OPERATIONAL PILLAR 03

Free Baby Formula, Clothes & Newborn Essentials

When a mother cannot produce milk due to life-threatening delivery trauma or illness, a newborn without formula faces acute malnutrition and death within days. In Malawi, the cost of commercial infant formula is far beyond the reach of impoverished households.

Wings provides a complete, 100% free infant care package covering the baby’s first 0 to 3 months of life. Everything required for the baby’s nutrition, thermal protection, and sanitation is provided without any cost to the family.

  • Infant Nutrition & Formula: Clinically certified baby formula supplied in consistent, adequate volume throughout the 0–3 month window.
  • Clothing & Thermal Protection: Soft warm baby clothes, knitted hats, and swaddles to protect against neonatal hypothermia.
  • Feeding Equipment & Hygiene Kits: Feeding bottles, sterilising containers, antiseptic soap, and umbilical cord hygiene care.
OPERATIONAL PILLAR 04

Essential Household Utilities: Electricity & Clean Water

Safe newborn bottle-feeding requires continuous access to safe, boiled water and electricity for sterilisation, refrigeration, and nocturnal care. In rural and impoverished peri-urban communities, utility disconnections or lack of clean water present a lethal infection risk to formula-fed infants.

Wings removes this barrier completely by directly paying electricity and water bills for qualifying families during the newborn’s 0 to 3 month critical period.

  • Clean Water Provision: Ensuring families have reliable access to uncontaminated water for boiling and formula preparation.
  • Electricity Utility Funding: Covering domestic power bills for heating, nighttime illumination, and thermal comfort.
  • 100% Free to the Mother: Eliminating catastrophic out-of-pocket costs so impoverished households are never forced to dilute formula or boil with contaminated water.
