SUPPORT LED BY WOMEN FOR MOTHERS

Comprehensive Maternal & Newborn Care Programmes

Our work in Malawi focuses on three interconnected programme pillars—Protection & Safety, Compassionate Care, and Family Support—reinforced by targeted interventions during the critical First 100 Days of Life.

PROGRAMME PILLAR 01

Protection & Safety for Vulnerable Newborns

Newborns born prematurely, with low birth weight, as twins or multiples, or into acute family crisis face immediate survival risks during their first weeks of life. Hypothermia, infection, and feeding difficulties can escalate rapidly once an infant leaves a hospital ward.

Our Protection & Safety programme establishes a protective safety net around high-risk infants and mothers—combining Kangaroo Mother Care (skin-to-skin thermal regulation) education, warm clothing bundles, and structured danger-sign monitoring.

  • Thermal Protection & Kangaroo Care: Equipping caregivers with knitted hats, wraps, and skin-to-skin guidance to prevent neonatal hypothermia.
  • High-Risk Infant Safeguarding: Prioritizing premature babies, multiples, and infants whose mothers face severe illness or crisis.
  • Early Danger-Sign Recognition: Helping mothers and families identify fever, breathing distress, or feeding issues early for rapid clinical referral.
PROGRAMME PILLAR 02

Compassionate Postnatal & Maternal Care

A newborn's wellbeing is inseparably linked to the health and recovery of their mother. In Malawi, many women return home after prolonged labor, emergency caesarean sections, or severe blood loss with limited access to postnatal nursing checkups.

Our Compassionate Care programme provides empathetic, women-led nursing accompaniment. We walk alongside recovering mothers with dignity and respect—checking surgical wounds, monitoring for postpartum infection or chronic anemia, and offering non-judgmental emotional support.

  • Postpartum Recovery Accompaniment: Routine maternal checkups following complicated deliveries or emergency surgical births.
  • Infection & Anemia Prevention: Hygiene supplies, wound-care guidance, and maternal nutrition education to support physical healing.
  • Respectful Mother-Centered Guidance: Creating a safe, welcoming environment where mothers feel heard and valued.
PROGRAMME PILLAR 03

Family & Extended Caregiver Support

In Malawi, maternal and newborn care is a shared family effort. When a mother is critically ill or passes away following childbirth, grandmothers, aunts, and extended relatives step forward immediately to care for the newborn—often travelling long distances on foot to seek help.

Our Family Support programme ensures that mothers, grandmothers, and caregivers are never left to navigate crisis alone. We provide practical infant care guidance, essential household supplies, and links to local maternal health clinics so babies can grow safely within their own families.

  • Grandmother & Kinship Backing: Supporting extended caregivers who raise orphaned or high-risk infants in rural and peri-urban communities.
  • Infant Feeding & Growth Guidance: Practical coaching on safe infant nutrition, hygiene, and regular weight checks.
  • Health Passport & Clinic Navigation: Helping families maintain routine immunizations and postnatal visits.
EARLY LIFE SUPPORT

Supporting the First 100 Days of Life

The first 100 days following birth represent the most decisive window for maternal healing and infant survival. Our four targeted interventions deliver immediate, practical care from the day a child is born.

Care Kits

Essential Packages

Newborn blankets, knitted hats, clean wraps, maternity hygiene items, soap, and cord-care essentials provided to vulnerable mothers at birth.

Postnatal Care

Maternity Assistance

Hands-on accompaniment through late pregnancy, safe delivery preparation, and structured postnatal recovery visits during the first 100 days.

Clinical Access

Maternal Health Clinics

Connecting mothers and infants with routine maternal consultations, infant weight monitoring, immunization schedules, and clinical checkups.

Rapid Response

Emergency Services

Urgent triage guidance and referral coordination when mothers or newborns experience acute postpartum or neonatal complications.
