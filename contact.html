COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how Wings coordinates maternal and newborn support across the UK and Malawi.

Where does Wings focus its maternal and newborn programmes?

Our frontline maternal and newborn care programmes focus on Malawi—supporting vulnerable newborns, postpartum mothers, and extended caregivers across hospital-linked and community settings—backed by our UK support and advocacy network.

What is included in an Essential Newborn Package?

Each package is tailored for the fragile First 100 Days of life and includes warm newborn blankets, knitted hats for thermal protection, clean baby wraps, soap, cord-care hygiene supplies, and postnatal guidance materials.

How does Wings support grandmothers and extended families?

When a mother experiences severe illness or loss following childbirth, grandmothers and aunts often step in to raise the infant. Our Family Support pillar provides these caregivers with practical infant supplies, nutrition guidance, and clinic checkup accompaniment.

Can healthcare professionals or NGOs partner with Wings?

Yes. We actively welcome collaboration with nurses, midwives, maternal clinics, and nonprofit organizations committed to advancing UN Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-being) in Malawi.
