FOUR WAYS TO SUPPORT

Direct Impact Pathways

Wings operates on a strict model: we don't support all babies; only cases where mum cannot care for the child. Here is how your support changes lives:

1. Sponsor Infant Formula & Essentials

Fund 0–3 months of high-grade formula, baby clothes, warm swaddles, bottles, and sterilising kits for infants of mothers in coma or with lactation failure.

2. Sponsor a Dedicated Private Nanny

Fund the wage of a trained, caring private nanny placed inside the household to provide round-the-clock newborn care while the mother recovers.

3. Fund Electricity & Clean Water Bills

Pay monthly utility bills directly so impoverished families can boil water, sterilise feeding bottles, and care for infants in warm, illuminated homes.

4. Hospital Clinical Referral Partnerships

We collaborate directly with Malawi government maternity hospital wards, obstetric doctors, and midwives to identify and vet eligible cases.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our operational vetting criteria, charity model, and how we deliver 100% free aid.

Do you support all newborns in Malawi?

No, we do not support all babies. Wings strictly supports cases where the mother cannot care for the child due to severe medical complications—such as coma, postpartum haemorrhage (PPH), severe eclampsia, lack of mental capacity, or lactation failure in extreme poverty.

What is included in the 0–3 month support package?

Our comprehensive support package includes certified infant formula, baby clothes, swaddles, feeding equipment, hygiene kits, dedicated private nannies placed in the household, and direct payment of domestic electricity and clean water bills—all 100% free of charge to mothers.

How are eligible mothers and babies identified?

We partner directly with government maternity hospital wards and referral facilities across Malawi. Obstetricians, midwives, and ward nurses work hand-in-hand with our team to verify and vet eligible cases according to our strict clinical criteria.

Who leads and operates Wings?

Wings is an international UK-based charity established and led by women passionate about supporting fellow mothers in need. We combine UK governance and philanthropic backing with experienced clinical and community partners in Malawi.
