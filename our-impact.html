UN SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOAL 3

Championing SDG 3 in Malawi

United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-being) establishes two urgent global priorities for maternal and infant survival:

  • SDG Target 3.1 (Maternal Mortality): Reducing preventable maternal deaths resulting from obstetric hemorrhage, postpartum infection, eclampsia, and obstructed labor.
  • SDG Target 3.2 (Newborn & Child Survival): Ending preventable deaths of newborns in their first 28 days of life and children under five years of age.

In Malawi, bridging the distance between rural villages, district health centers, and central referral hospitals—and following up with mothers and grandmothers once they return home—is decisive in achieving both targets.

CONTINUUM OF CARE

Why Early-Life Timing Changes Everything

Health outcomes for both mother and child depend on uninterrupted support across four connected stages—from late pregnancy through the first 1,000 days.

Stage 01 • Birth

Safe Delivery & Immediate Warmth

Preparing mothers with clean delivery supplies, warm newborn wraps, and skilled birth attendance to prevent birth asphyxia and neonatal hypothermia.

Stage 02 • Days 1–28

The Fragile Neonatal Window

Close monitoring of premature, low-birth-weight, and twin newborns through Kangaroo Mother Care, cord hygiene, and early infection screening.

Stage 03 • First 100 Days

Postnatal Recovery & Stabilization

Supporting maternal physical healing after complicated births while establishing steady infant weight gain, immunizations, and family stability.

Stage 04 • First 1,000 Days

Long-Term Child Thriving

Accompanying families and grandmothers with ongoing nutrition guidance, clinic checkups, and developmental protection through early childhood.

EVIDENCE & ACCOUNTABILITY

How We Track & Verify Outcomes

Credible nonprofit work requires honest, verifiable monitoring. We track four practical indicators across our programmes to ensure our support translates into real health outcomes for families.

Indicator 01

Infant Weight & Growth

Tracking steady weight gain and thermal stability among premature, low-birth-weight, and high-risk newborns during their first 100 days.

Indicator 02

Postnatal Maternal Healing

Monitoring maternal recovery after complicated labor or C-sections, ensuring timely follow-up for infection or anemia.

Indicator 04
