WHO WE ARE & OUR PURPOSE

Women Supporting Mothers: Giving Vulnerable Newborns a Fighting Chance

Wings is an international UK-based charity established and led by women passionate about supporting fellow mothers in need. Our aim is to give a real chance to newborns who are critically vulnerable right after birth, through no fault of their own.

OUR CRITICAL MANDATE

We Don’t Support All Babies — Only Where Mum Cannot Care

To ensure zero aid dilution and maximum survival impact, Wings operates with a clear, honest principle: we don’t support all babies. We focus exclusively on the most critical emergencies where the mother cannot care for her newborn.

These are babies whose mothers are facing life-threatening medical conditions often compounded by extreme poverty:

  • Maternal Coma & Critical ICU Admission: Mothers unconscious or battling for survival following obstructed labor or traumatic surgical birth.
  • Postpartum Hemorrhage & Eclampsia: Mothers severely weakened by massive blood loss, seizures, or physical incapacitation.
  • Lack of Mental Capacity: Mothers experiencing severe postpartum psychosis, intellectual disability, or acute mental health trauma preventing safe newborn care.
  • Complete Lactation Failure in Destitute Homes: Mothers biologically unable to produce breastmilk, from homes too poor to afford commercial infant formula.
OUR OPERATIONAL MODEL

Vetted Through Government Hospitals in Malawi

We don’t guess who needs help. Wings works directly with government hospitals in Malawi. When a patient suffers severe labor complications, coma, or postpartum trauma, maternity ward doctors and head nurses alert our team.

Our specialized field team works hand-in-hand with hospital staff to vet and verify eligible cases against our strict clinical criteria. Once verified, Wings immediately deploys our complete 0–3 month care package—ensuring the infant is protected from day one, entirely free of charge to the family.

THE FIELD REALITY

Why We Are Working in Malawi

Malawi has one of the highest maternal mortality and neonatal mortality rates in the world, far exceeding United Nations targets. Wings focuses on tackling two decisive drivers behind these tragic losses.

Driver 01 • Systemic Challenge

Poverty & Inequality

Widespread poverty, deep food insecurity, and limited education exacerbate health risks. When a mother cannot breastfeed due to illness, her family cannot afford commercial infant formula, clean bottled water, or electricity—forcing newborns into rapid starvation or fatal diarrheal illness.

Driver 02 • Systemic Challenge

Inadequate Prenatal & Postnatal Care

Insufficient antenatal visits, lack of emergency surgical facilities, and sparse postnatal checkups contribute to catastrophic delivery complications like uncontrolled PPH and eclampsia. Once discharged, incapacitated mothers have no home nursing safety net.

The Wings Solution • 0–3 Months

Complete Life-Support Package

We cover 100% of baby formula, clothes, essentials, home electricity, and water bills for the first 3 months. By funding utilities, we make safe formula preparation, bottle boiling, and warm heating possible.

The Wings Solution • Hands-on Care

Private Nannies in the Home

An incapacitated mother cannot feed or monitor her newborn. Wings recruits, trains, and funds dedicated private nannies to provide 24/7 care directly in the home while the mother recovers or receives clinical treatment.
