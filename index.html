UK-Based, Women-Led Charity Operating in Malawi

Giving Vulnerable Newborns a Chance at Life

Wings is an international UK-based charity led by women, dedicated to supporting fellow mothers in desperate need. We provide comprehensive, 100% free care for newborns (0–3 months) whose mothers cannot care for them due to coma, severe postpartum hemorrhage, eclampsia, lack of mental capacity, or inability to produce milk in extreme poverty.

HOW WE WORK WITH GOVERNMENT HOSPITALS

Strict Criteria. Comprehensive Care. Zero Cost.

Wings partners directly with government hospitals in Malawi. Clinical staff identify vulnerable cases, and our dedicated team vets and verifies each family to ensure emergency resources reach newborns who have no other safety net.

Hospital-Vetted Cases

We work alongside doctors and maternity ward staff to verify cases where the mother is in a coma, critically ill, lacking mental capacity, or suffering severe postpartum complications.

ALIGNING WITH GLOBAL GOALS

Championing SDG 3 in Malawi

Our work is directly aligned with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 3: Good Health and Well-being, targeting the reduction of maternal and newborn mortality in Malawi. Malawi is actively working toward halving maternal and newborn deaths and stillbirths.

Wings tackles two decisive drivers behind these high mortality rates:

Poverty & Inequality Widespread poverty and food insecurity prevent destitute families from purchasing life-saving formula and utilities when mothers cannot nurse.
Inadequate Postnatal Care Insufficient postnatal follow-ups and emergency care leave vulnerable newborns without monitoring after hospital discharge.

Targeting the 0–3 Month Survival Cliff

Bridging hospital discharge and home survival so no infant in Malawi is lost when a mother suffers life-threatening delivery complications.
